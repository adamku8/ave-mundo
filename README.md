## About

AVE MUNDO partners with founders and investors to build long-term value through technology and people.

We are an investment holding focused on e-commerce and SaaS companies in the EU. The main goal is to accelerate their growth through expertise and mutual synergies.

**We are not** an accelerator, an agency, or a VC fund. We are a platform designed to compound value across a portfolio.
