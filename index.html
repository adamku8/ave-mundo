What is AVE MUNDO

AI, ecommerce and SaaS.

AVE MUNDO is an investment holding focused on e-commerce and SaaS companies in the EU. The main goal is to accelerate their growth through expertise and mutual synergies.

We build a shared ecosystem the portfolio actually uses — the kind that moves a roadmap forward, not the kind that lives in a deck.

  • Know-howStrategy, go-to-market, product. Operator depth across the portfolio.

  • AI & automationScalable execution. Less ops friction. More compounding.
