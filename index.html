What is AVE MUNDO

AI, ecommerce and SaaS.

AVE MUNDO is an investment holding focused on e-commerce and SaaS companies in the EU. The main goal is to accelerate their growth through expertise and mutual synergies.

We build a shared ecosystem the portfolio actually uses — the kind that moves a roadmap forward, not the kind that lives in a deck. Capital is aligned and long-horizon, built for compounding rather than quick exits. Know-how spans strategy, go-to-market, and product, with operator depth across the portfolio. Data and reporting drive KPI decisions through shared dashboards and honest numbers. And AI and automation make execution scalable — less ops friction, more compounding.
