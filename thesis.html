Investment Thesis

Portfolio logic over single bets.

What we look for, what we don't, and how we partner with founders to compound long-term value.

What we believe

Most great companies aren't built by chasing exits — they're built by compounding value over decades.

Venture funds operate on a fixed lifecycle: deploy capital, push for growth at any cost, force an exit within 7-10 years. That works for some categories, but it pushes founders to optimize for narrative over substance, fundraising over revenue, and short-term traction over long-term moats.

AVE MUNDO is built on the opposite premise. An investment holding with no fund-cycle pressure. Founders keep operational autonomy. Capital is aligned and long-horizon. The portfolio shares operator know-how, data infrastructure, and AI tooling. Each company strengthens the next.

The result: a system for repeatable company building, not a series of independent bets.

What we look for

Four signals we look for in every conversation with founders.

Global by default

Designed for markets beyond the local one from day one. Pricing, product, and language strategy that doesn't break when you cross borders.

Software- and data-driven

Software at the core, not as a layer on top of services. Data accumulates with usage and becomes a moat.

Built to scale

Architecture and team structure ready to grow beyond ten engineers and one geography. Not a hyperlocal lifestyle business.

Built for long-term value

Compounding moats, not short-term hype. Founders who think in decades, not quarters.

What we don't back

Pre-product / pre-revenue

We back post-PMF companies. Bring traction, paying customers, or a clear path to monetisation.

Hyperlocal-only models

Businesses where the moat depends on a single city or country. We invest in scalable infrastructure.

Pure services / agencies

Time-for-money businesses without product leverage. We back software with services, not the inverse.

Hype-driven, no moat

If your competitive advantage disappears when GPT-X drops, the moat isn't there. We invest in compounding data and execution, not narratives.

Where we focus

Sectors
E-commerce infrastructure, SaaS, AI / automation, cross-border commerce
Geography
European Union — preference for CEE, but not exclusive
Stage
Post-PMF. Traction or revenue required

What we provide

More than capital. The portfolio actually uses what we build.

Capital. Aligned, long-horizon, built for compounding rather than quick exits. No quarterly pressure, no fund-cycle deadlines.

Operator know-how. Two decades of building, scaling, and exiting startups across Central Europe. Strategy, go-to-market, product — at the depth only operators can offer.

Shared infrastructure. Data, reporting, AI, and automation tooling that the portfolio actually uses. Not a deck of resources promised at term sheet stage and forgotten by the next board.

Go-to-market support across EU and beyond. Distribution, partnerships, hiring help where it moves the needle.

Honest numbers. Shared dashboards, KPI-driven decisions. We optimize for direction, speed, and compounding effects — not narrative.

How we work with founders

Founders keep operational autonomy. We don't run companies — we equip founders to run theirs better.

We focus on building an asset, not just a product. Long-term horizons. No quarterly pressure. The conversation is about direction and decisions, not status updates.

One conversation is enough to know if there's a fit. We aim to respond to every pitch within 5 business days — clear yes, clear no, or a question. No ghosting.
